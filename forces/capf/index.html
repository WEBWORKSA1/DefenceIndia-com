Central Armed Police Forces

BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, SSB and Assam Rifles under the Ministry of Home Affairs — over 10 lakh personnel guarding borders, internal security and critical infrastructure.

Strength~10 lakh across six forces
ForcesBSF (Pakistan & Bangladesh borders), ITBP (China), SSB (Nepal & Bhutan), CRPF (internal security), CISF (installations & airports), Assam Rifles (Myanmar; MHA-controlled, Army-officered)
EntriesUPSC CAPF Assistant Commandant, SSC GD Constable, SSC CPO Sub-Inspector, departmental
NoteEx-Agniveers get 10% reservation and age relaxation in CAPF constable recruitment

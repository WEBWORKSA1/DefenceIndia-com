Force profile

Indian Navy

A blue-water navy of ~150 ships and submarines and ~300 aircraft, organised into Western, Eastern and Southern Naval Commands with two carrier battle groups.

Strength~70,000 personnel
Fleet2 carriers, 12 destroyers, 13 frigates, 18 submarines (incl. 2 SSBN), 20 corvettes, P-8I & MH-60R aviation
CommandsWestern (Mumbai), Eastern (Visakhapatnam), Southern (Kochi), Andaman & Nicobar (tri-service)
EntriesNDA, CDS (INA), 10+2 B.Tech Cadet, SSC Officer (all branches), Agniveer SSR/MR

NDA & NA Examination

Age 16.5–19.5 · Officer

The National Defence Academy exam is the primary route for 12th-pass candidates to become officers in all three services after three years at Khadakwasla and a year at the respective service academy.

Combined Defence Services Examination
CDS

Combined Defence Services Examination

Age 19–25 · Officer

CDS is the graduate-entry gateway to permanent commission via IMA, INA and AFA, and short service commission via OTA Chennai.

Agniveer — Indian Navy (SSR & MR)

Age 17.5–21 · Other Ranks

Senior Secondary Recruit (SSR) and Matric Recruit (MR) entries for sailors under Agnipath; women are inducted in both.

Indian Navy SSC Officer (Executive, Technical, Education)

Age 19–25 · Officer

The Navy's graduate short-service commission covering General Service, Pilot, Observer, ATC, Naval Armament Inspection, Logistics, Education and Technical branches.

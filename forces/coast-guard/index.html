Indian Coast Guard

Under the Ministry of Defence, the Coast Guard secures 7,500 km of coastline and a 2.4 million sq km EEZ with ~150 ships and 75 aircraft — the fourth-largest coast guard in the world.

Strength~15,000 personnel
RegionsWest (Mumbai), East (Chennai), North-East (Kolkata), Andaman & Nicobar, North-West (Gandhinagar)
RolesMaritime search and rescue, anti-smuggling, pollution response, fisheries protection, coastal security
EntriesAssistant Commandant (CGCAT), Navik GD/DB, Yantrik

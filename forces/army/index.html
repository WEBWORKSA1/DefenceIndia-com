Indian Army

World's second-largest standing army: ~1.2 million active personnel across 7 commands, 14 corps and ~40 divisions. Chief of the Army Staff heads it from Integrated HQ, New Delhi.

Strength~1.2 million active, ~9.6 lakh reserve
CommandsNorthern, Western, South Western, Southern, Central, Eastern, Training (ARTRAC)
Key equipmentT-90, T-72, Arjun; K9 Vajra, Dhanush, ATAGS, Pinaka; Apache, Prachand, Dhruv/Rudra; BrahMos, Akash
EntriesNDA, CDS, TES, TGC, SSC Tech, NCC Special, JAG, Agniveer, JCO Religious Teacher, TA

How to join the Indian Army

All entries →
NDA & NA Examination
NDA

NDA & NA Examination

Age 16.5–19.5 · Officer

The National Defence Academy exam is the primary route for 12th-pass candidates to become officers in all three services after three years at Khadakwasla and a year at the respective service academy.

Combined Defence Services Examination
CDS

Combined Defence Services Examination

Age 19–25 · Officer

CDS is the graduate-entry gateway to permanent commission via IMA, INA and AFA, and short service commission via OTA Chennai.

Agniveer — Indian Army (GD, Technical, Clerk/SKT, Tradesman)
Agniveer Army

Agniveer — Indian Army (GD, Technical, Clerk/SKT, Tradesman)

Age 17.5–21 · Other Ranks

Agnipath's four-year enrolment for soldiers; 25% retained as regular cadre after four years. The single largest defence recruitment by volume.

Technical Entry Scheme (10+2 TES)
TES

Technical Entry Scheme (10+2 TES)

Age 16.5–19.5 · Officer

Direct engineering-officer entry for 12th pass; five years of training leads to a B.Tech and permanent commission.

Technical Graduate Course
TGC

Technical Graduate Course

Age 20–27 · Officer

Permanent-commission entry for engineering graduates with one-year training at IMA; no written exam.

Short Service Commission (Technical) — Men & Women
SSC Tech

Short Service Commission (Technical) — Men & Women

Age 20–27 · Officer

Short service commission (10 + 4 years) for engineers, with an option to convert to permanent commission.

NCC Special Entry Scheme
NCC Entry

NCC Special Entry Scheme

Age 19–25 · Officer

Senior-division NCC cadets with a 'C' certificate skip the written test and go straight to SSB for short service commission.

Territorial Army Officer
TA Officer

Territorial Army Officer

Age 18–42 · Officer

The 'citizen's army' allows working professionals to serve part-time as commissioned officers, with embodiment for training and operations.

Judge Advocate General (JAG) Entry
JAG

Judge Advocate General (JAG) Entry

Age 21–27 · Officer

Short service commission for law graduates in the Army's legal branch; shortlisting now uses CLAT PG scores.

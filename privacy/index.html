Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

What we collect

Information you submit through forms (name, contact details, qualification, company details and messages); technical data such as IP address, device and pages viewed via analytics; and cookie identifiers used for analytics and advertising.

How we use it

To respond to enquiries, deliver counselling callbacks and vendor services you request, send newsletters you subscribe to, run contests, improve the site and serve advertising. Counselling and vendor enquiries may be shared with the specific partner (coaching institute, mentor or industry contact) needed to fulfil your request. We do not sell personal data.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google Analytics and Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this or other websites. You may opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Affiliate links (e.g. Amazon Associates) may set cookies to attribute purchases.

Form processing

Forms are delivered via a third-party form relay service to our inbox over HTTPS. We retain submissions for as long as needed to serve the request and comply with law.

Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your data, or unsubscribe at any time, via the contact page. We comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and honour GDPR requests from EU residents.

Children

Users under 18 should use the site with parental guidance; contest entries from minors require guardian consent.

Changes

Updates are posted on this page with a revised date.

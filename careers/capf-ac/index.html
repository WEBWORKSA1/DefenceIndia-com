Preparation tips

  • Paper II (essay, précis, comprehension) is the differentiator — practise writing.
  • PET: 100 m in 16 s (men) / 18 s (women), 800 m, long jump, shot put.
  • Age relaxation of 5 years for SC/ST, 3 for OBC.

Frequently asked

What is the age limit for CAPF AC?

20 to 25 years, calculated as on the date specified in the notification (usually the first day of the month the course commences).

Is there negative marking?

Most UPSC and service-conducted objective papers carry one-third negative marking; check the specific notification.

How many attempts are allowed?

There is no attempt limit — only the age band applies.

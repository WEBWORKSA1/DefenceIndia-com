Preparation tips

  • Class 6 entry: age 10-12 as on 31 March of admission year.
  • Girls admitted in Class 6 across all Sainik Schools since 2021-22.
  • RIMC has a separate exam (June & December) with state quotas.

Frequently asked

What is the age limit for AISSEE / RIMC?

10 to 13 years, calculated as on the date specified in the notification (usually the first day of the month the course commences).

Is there negative marking?

Most UPSC and service-conducted objective papers carry one-third negative marking; check the specific notification.

How many attempts are allowed?

There is no attempt limit — only the age band applies.

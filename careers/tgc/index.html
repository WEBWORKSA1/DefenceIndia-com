Preparation tips

  • Cut-off is by branch percentage — keep CGPA conversion certificate ready.
  • Stream-wise vacancies change every course.
  • Ante-date seniority is granted for the engineering degree.

Frequently asked

What is the age limit for TGC?

20 to 27 years, calculated as on the date specified in the notification (usually the first day of the month the course commences).

Is there negative marking?

Most UPSC and service-conducted objective papers carry one-third negative marking; check the specific notification.

How many attempts are allowed?

There is no attempt limit — only the age band applies.

Dates shown are indicative based on past cycles. Always confirm with the official notification before applying.

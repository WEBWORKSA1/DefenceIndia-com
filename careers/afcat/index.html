Air Force Common Admission Test

Air Force (Flying, Ground Duty Technical & Non-Technical) · Twice a year (February & August) · Next cycle: 2027-02-14 ()

AFCAT is the IAF's own entry for short service commission in Flying branch and permanent/short service in Ground Duty branches.

Age limit20 – 26 years
GenderMen & Women
QualificationGraduate 60% (Physics & Maths at 12th for Flying); BE/BTech for Technical
Selection processOnline AFCAT (100 Q, 300 marks) + EKT for Technical → AFSB → Medical
NotificationDecember / June
Pay / stipendFlying Officer — Level 10 + MSP + Flying allowance
Official websiteafcat.cdac.in

Preparation tips

  • Flying branch age is 20-24; Ground duty 20-26 at course commencement.
  • EKT is additional for technical branch — engineering fundamentals.
  • AFSB is conducted at Dehradun, Mysuru, Gandhinagar and Varanasi.

Frequently asked

What is the age limit for AFCAT?

20 to 26 years, calculated as on the date specified in the notification (usually the first day of the month the course commences).

Is there negative marking?

Most UPSC and service-conducted objective papers carry one-third negative marking; check the specific notification.

How many attempts are allowed?

There is no attempt limit — only the age band applies.

Dates shown are indicative based on past cycles. Always confirm with the official notification before applying.

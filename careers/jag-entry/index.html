Preparation tips

  • Equal vacancies for men and women in recent courses.
  • CLAT PG score of the current year only is accepted.
  • Post-commission you can practise military law across courts martial.

Frequently asked

What is the age limit for JAG?

21 to 27 years, calculated as on the date specified in the notification (usually the first day of the month the course commences).

Is there negative marking?

Most UPSC and service-conducted objective papers carry one-third negative marking; check the specific notification.

How many attempts are allowed?

There is no attempt limit — only the age band applies.

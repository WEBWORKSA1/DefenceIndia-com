Coast Guard · Twice a year · Next cycle: 2027-03-01 ()

Preparation tips

  • Technical branch age is 21-27 (vs 21-25 GD).
  • PP&DT is identical to SSB screening — same preparation applies.
  • Height 157 cm minimum; eyesight standards stricter for GD.

Frequently asked

What is the age limit for ICG AC?

21 to 25 years, calculated as on the date specified in the notification (usually the first day of the month the course commences).

Is there negative marking?

Most UPSC and service-conducted objective papers carry one-third negative marking; check the specific notification.

How many attempts are allowed?

There is no attempt limit — only the age band applies.

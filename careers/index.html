Officer entries

NDA & NA Examination
NDA

NDA & NA Examination

UPSC · Age 16.5–19.5 · Twice a year (April & September)

The National Defence Academy exam is the primary route for 12th-pass candidates to become officers in all three services after three years at Khadakwasla and a year at the respective service academy.

Combined Defence Services Examination
CDS

Combined Defence Services Examination

UPSC · Age 19–25 · Twice a year (April & September)

CDS is the graduate-entry gateway to permanent commission via IMA, INA and AFA, and short service commission via OTA Chennai.

Air Force Common Admission Test
AFCAT

Air Force Common Admission Test

Indian Air Force · Age 20–26 · Twice a year (February & August)

AFCAT is the IAF's own entry for short service commission in Flying branch and permanent/short service in Ground Duty branches.

SSB Interview (Services Selection Board)
SSB

SSB Interview (Services Selection Board)

Army / Navy / Air Force · Age 16.5–42 · Continuous — after every officer written / shortlist

The five-day SSB assesses 15 Officer-Like Qualities through psychologist, GTO and interviewing officer. Recommendation rate hovers around 5-10%.

Technical Entry Scheme (10+2 TES)
TES

Technical Entry Scheme (10+2 TES)

Indian Army · Age 16.5–19.5 · Twice a year

Direct engineering-officer entry for 12th pass; five years of training leads to a B.Tech and permanent commission.

Technical Graduate Course
TGC

Technical Graduate Course

Indian Army · Age 20–27 · Twice a year

Permanent-commission entry for engineering graduates with one-year training at IMA; no written exam.

Short Service Commission (Technical) — Men & Women
SSC Tech

Short Service Commission (Technical) — Men & Women

Indian Army · Age 20–27 · Twice a year

Short service commission (10 + 4 years) for engineers, with an option to convert to permanent commission.

NCC Special Entry Scheme
NCC Entry

NCC Special Entry Scheme

Indian Army · Age 19–25 · Twice a year

Senior-division NCC cadets with a 'C' certificate skip the written test and go straight to SSB for short service commission.

Indian Navy SSC Officer (Executive, Technical, Education)
Navy SSC

Indian Navy SSC Officer (Executive, Technical, Education)

Indian Navy · Age 19–25 · Twice a year (Jan & Jun courses)

The Navy's graduate short-service commission covering General Service, Pilot, Observer, ATC, Naval Armament Inspection, Logistics, Education and Technical branches.

10+2 (B.Tech) Cadet Entry — Indian Navy
Navy B.Tech Cadet

10+2 (B.Tech) Cadet Entry — Indian Navy

Indian Navy · Age 16.5–19.5 · Twice a year

Four-year B.Tech at the Indian Naval Academy, Ezhimala, leading to permanent commission in Executive or Technical branches.

CAPF Assistant Commandant (UPSC)
CAPF AC

CAPF Assistant Commandant (UPSC)

UPSC · Age 20–25 · Annual (August)

Group A entry into the Central Armed Police Forces — India's largest uniformed employer after the Army.

Territorial Army Officer
TA Officer

Territorial Army Officer

Territorial Army (Indian Army) · Age 18–42 · Annual

The 'citizen's army' allows working professionals to serve part-time as commissioned officers, with embodiment for training and operations.

Indian Coast Guard Assistant Commandant (CGCAT)
ICG AC

Indian Coast Guard Assistant Commandant (CGCAT)

Indian Coast Guard · Age 21–25 · Twice a year

Officer entry into the Coast Guard's General Duty, Commercial Pilot, Technical and Law branches through the CGCAT.

Judge Advocate General (JAG) Entry
JAG

Judge Advocate General (JAG) Entry

Indian Army · Age 21–27 · Twice a year

Short service commission for law graduates in the Army's legal branch; shortlisting now uses CLAT PG scores.

Soldier, sailor, airman & other-rank entries

Agniveer — Indian Army (GD, Technical, Clerk/SKT, Tradesman)
Agniveer Army

Agniveer — Indian Army (GD, Technical, Clerk/SKT, Tradesman)

Indian Army · Age 17.5–21

Agnipath's four-year enrolment for soldiers; 25% retained as regular cadre after four years. The single largest defence recruitment by volume.

Agniveervayu — Indian Air Force
Agniveervayu

Agniveervayu — Indian Air Force

Indian Air Force · Age 17.5–21

The IAF's Agnipath intake for airmen-equivalent Agniveervayu, tested through the online STAR examination and a physical fitness test.

Agniveer — Indian Navy (SSR & MR)
Agniveer Navy

Agniveer — Indian Navy (SSR & MR)

Indian Navy · Age 17.5–21

Senior Secondary Recruit (SSR) and Matric Recruit (MR) entries for sailors under Agnipath; women are inducted in both.

Indian Coast Guard Navik (GD / DB) & Yantrik
ICG Navik

Indian Coast Guard Navik (GD / DB) & Yantrik

Indian Coast Guard · Age 18–22

Enrolled-personnel entry for the Coast Guard — regular service, not Agnipath.

SSC GD Constable (BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, SSB, Assam Rifles)
SSC GD

SSC GD Constable (BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, SSB, Assam Rifles)

Staff Selection Commission · Age 18–23

The largest single uniformed recruitment in India — vacancies routinely exceed 40,000.

Sainik School Entrance (AISSEE) & RIMC
AISSEE / RIMC

Sainik School Entrance (AISSEE) & RIMC

NTA / RIMC Dehradun · Age 10–13

Sainik Schools and RIMC are the traditional pipeline into NDA — a large share of NDA cadets come from these 33+ schools.

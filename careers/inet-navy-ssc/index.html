Preparation tips

  • Different branches have different age bands — read the table, not the headline.
  • Pilot/Observer require CPSS at the SSB.
  • Applications open on the Navy portal for about two weeks — set an alert.

Frequently asked

What is the age limit for Navy SSC?

19 to 25 years, calculated as on the date specified in the notification (usually the first day of the month the course commences).

Is there negative marking?

Most UPSC and service-conducted objective papers carry one-third negative marking; check the specific notification.

How many attempts are allowed?

There is no attempt limit — only the age band applies.

Dates shown are indicative based on past cycles. Always confirm with the official notification before applying.

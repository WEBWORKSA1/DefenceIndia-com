Territorial Army Officer

Army (part-time) · Annual · Next cycle: 2027-06-01 ()

The 'citizen's army' allows working professionals to serve part-time as commissioned officers, with embodiment for training and operations.

Age limit18 – 42 years
GenderMen & Women
QualificationGraduate + gainfully employed (self-employed accepted)
Selection processWritten (Reasoning & Elementary Maths; GK & English) → PIB Interview → SSB → Medical
NotificationMay / June
Pay / stipendLieutenant pay while embodied
Official websitejointerritorialarmy.gov.in

Preparation tips

  • Proof of gainful employment is mandatory at the PIB stage.
  • Written exam pattern is lighter than CDS; PIB screening is the real hurdle.
  • Popular second attempt route for CDS over-age candidates.

Frequently asked

What is the age limit for TA Officer?

18 to 42 years, calculated as on the date specified in the notification (usually the first day of the month the course commences).

Is there negative marking?

Most UPSC and service-conducted objective papers carry one-third negative marking; check the specific notification.

How many attempts are allowed?

There is no attempt limit — only the age band applies.

Dates shown are indicative based on past cycles. Always confirm with the official notification before applying.

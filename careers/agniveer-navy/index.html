Agniveer — Indian Navy (SSR & MR)

Navy · Twice a year · Next cycle: 2027-01-20 ()

Senior Secondary Recruit (SSR) and Matric Recruit (MR) entries for sailors under Agnipath; women are inducted in both.

Age limit17.5 – 21 years
GenderMen & Women
QualificationClass 12 with Maths & Physics (SSR); Class 10 (MR)
Selection processINET-Agniveer online exam → PFT → Medical
NotificationMay / November
Pay / stipend₹30,000–₹40,000 per month + Seva Nidhi
Official websitejoinindiannavy.gov.in

Preparation tips

  • SSR shortlisting is based on 12th marks before the exam — keep your score sheet ready.
  • MR covers chef, steward and hygienist roles.
  • PFT: 1.6 km run in 6 min 30 s, 20 squats, 15 push-ups.

Frequently asked

What is the age limit for Agniveer Navy?

17.5 to 21 years, calculated as on the date specified in the notification (usually the first day of the month the course commences).

Is there negative marking?

Most UPSC and service-conducted objective papers carry one-third negative marking; check the specific notification.

How many attempts are allowed?

There is no attempt limit — only the age band applies.

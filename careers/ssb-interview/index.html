Preparation tips

  • Practise OIR verbal & non-verbal reasoning for speed — screening-out is ~60%.
  • Write a genuine PIQ form; every interview question flows from it.
  • Physical fitness matters in GTO tasks; run and do obstacle training beforehand.

Frequently asked

What is the age limit for SSB?

16.5 to 42 years, calculated as on the date specified in the notification (usually the first day of the month the course commences).

Is there negative marking?

Most UPSC and service-conducted objective papers carry one-third negative marking; check the specific notification.

How many attempts are allowed?

There is no attempt limit — only the age band applies.

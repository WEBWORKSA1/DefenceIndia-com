Agniveer — Indian Army (GD, Technical, Clerk/SKT, Tradesman)

Army · Annual cycle (registration usually Feb–Mar) · Next cycle: 2027-02-15 ()

Agnipath's four-year enrolment for soldiers; 25% retained as regular cadre after four years. The single largest defence recruitment by volume.

Age limit17.5 – 21 years
GenderMen & Women (select categories)
QualificationClass 10 (GD/Tradesman) or Class 12 (Technical/Clerk)
Selection processOnline CEE → Rally (PFT, PMT) → Medical → Merit
NotificationFebruary
Pay / stipend₹30,000 (Yr1) rising to ₹40,000 (Yr4) + Seva Nidhi ₹10.04 lakh at exit

Frequently asked

What is the age limit for Agniveer Army?

17.5 to 21 years, calculated as on the date specified in the notification (usually the first day of the month the course commences).

Is there negative marking?

Most UPSC and service-conducted objective papers carry one-third negative marking; check the specific notification.

How many attempts are allowed?

There is no attempt limit — only the age band applies.

Technical Entry Scheme (10+2 TES)

Army (Technical arms) · Twice a year · Next cycle: 2027-05-20 ()

Direct engineering-officer entry for 12th pass; five years of training leads to a B.Tech and permanent commission.

Age limit16.5 – 19.5 years
GenderMen
QualificationClass 12 with PCM 60% aggregate + JEE Main appeared
Selection processShortlist on JEE Main rank → SSB → Medical

Pay / stipendStipend during training; Lt on commissioning (Level 10)

Preparation tips

  • No written exam — JEE Main score is the gate.
  • Very limited vacancies (~90) — cut-offs are high.
  • SSB call letters go by JEE rank order.

Frequently asked

What is the age limit for TES?

16.5 to 19.5 years, calculated as on the date specified in the notification (usually the first day of the month the course commences).

Is there negative marking?

Most UPSC and service-conducted objective papers carry one-third negative marking; check the specific notification.

How many attempts are allowed?

There is no attempt limit — only the age band applies.

Dates shown are indicative based on past cycles. Always confirm with the official notification before applying.

Preparation tips

  • Negative marking of 0.25 — accuracy first.
  • PET: 5 km in 24 min (men), 1.6 km in 8.5 min (women).
  • Force preference order in the form decides allotment.

Frequently asked

What is the age limit for SSC GD?

18 to 23 years, calculated as on the date specified in the notification (usually the first day of the month the course commences).

Is there negative marking?

Most UPSC and service-conducted objective papers carry one-third negative marking; check the specific notification.

How many attempts are allowed?

There is no attempt limit — only the age band applies.

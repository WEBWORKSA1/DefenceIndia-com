About DefenceIndia.com

DefenceIndia.com is an independent media and information platform covering India's armed forces, defence careers and the defence industry. We serve three communities that rarely get one home: the millions of young Indians preparing for uniformed careers, the serving and veteran community, and the companies building Make in India in defence.

What we do

  • Explainers and analysis on the services, procurement, reform and the military balance.
  • A careers hub with every entry route, live tools and free counselling.
  • An equipment database and comparison tools.
  • Industry intelligence — directory, procurement tracker and start-up coverage.

How we are funded

Advertising (including Google AdSense), sponsored content that is always labelled, lead-generation partnerships with coaching institutes and industry, affiliate links, reader support and contest sponsorships. Editorial coverage is never for sale. See our editorial policy.

Independence

We are not affiliated with the Ministry of Defence, any armed force, or any government body. We do not publish classified or restricted information and we respect operational security. See the disclaimer.

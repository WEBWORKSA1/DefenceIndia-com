Editorial Policy

Accuracy and sourcing

We rely on official releases (PIB, MoD, service HQs, UPSC/SSC notifications), primary documents, and reputable reporting. Figures are attributed; estimates are marked as such. Explainers carry a date and are refreshed when facts change.

Independence

Advertisers and sponsors have no influence on editorial coverage. Sponsored content and affiliate links are labelled. Staff do not trade on non-public information about listed defence companies.

Corrections

Material errors are corrected in the article with a note. Submit a correction via the contact page with the URL and the correct information and source.

Operational security

We do not publish troop movements, exact deployments, or any restricted information, and we decline material that appears classified.

Comments and community

Discussion happens on our WhatsApp, Telegram and social channels under their rules; abuse, misinformation and OPSEC violations are removed.

AI and tools

Automated tools assist with research and drafting; every published claim is reviewed by a human editor.

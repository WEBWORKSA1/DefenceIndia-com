# Competitive Research — 40 Defence-Niche Websites (September 2026)

Three parallel audits covered global defence media, Indian defence media, and careers/industry/reference sites. Sites that blocked fetching were profiled via search and mirrors and are marked *.

## A. Global defence media (13)
janes.com, defensenews.com, breakingdefense.com, twz.com (The War Zone), armyrecognition.com, navalnews.com, army-technology.com, shephardmedia.com, defenceweb.co.za, defence-blog.com, military.com*, taskandpurpose.com, eurasiantimes.com

**Top features by frequency × value:** domain-based nav with mega-menus (12/13); hero + category card grids (13/13); newsletter capture in 3–4 placements (12/13); display ad slots leaderboard/in-grid/sidebar (11/13); labelled sponsored content (10/13); search with filters (13/13); video section / branded show (10/13); most-read sidebar (8/13); analysis separated from news (11/13); expo/event coverage hubs (8/13); bylines + timestamps (12/13); advertise/media-kit page (12/13); regional taxonomy (7/13); equipment database with spec sheets (4/13 — highest differentiation); press-release submission (4/13); company directory/zones (5/13); webinars (4/13); premium tier/Patreon (5/13); jobs board (4/13); visual trackers (2/13); gated reports (4/13); merch (3/13); trust stats on homepage (2/13); dark mode (5/13).

**Best CTA patterns:** multi-placement newsletter; "Got a tip?" form; gated report download; trust-stat hero with one CTA; press-release submission; sponsored company zones; webinar registration; Patreon/member tier before hard paywall.

## B. Indian defence media (14)
bharatshakti.in, idrw.org, indiandefencenews.in (dead), defencexp.com, forceindia.net, stratpost.com, livefistdefence.com, delhidefencereview.com, financialexpress.com/defence*, theprint.in/defence, spslandforces.com, indiandefensenews.in, defenceaviationpost.com (hijacked domain), alphadefense.in*

**Common:** Army/Navy/Air Force silos; India/neighbours/world geography; procurement beat drives traffic; veteran opinion columns; YouTube embeds (8/12 live sites); AdSense on blog tier, direct-sold OEM banners (L&T, Adani, TASL, Solar) on credible tier; "Advertise" = an email address; subscriptions only where print exists; donations via Buy Me a Coffee (idrw); merch (ThePrint).

**Gaps exploited by this build:** no structured databases (equipment, procurement tracker, tender calendar, PSU dashboard); no real newsletter product or podcast; no tip/press-release/jobs forms despite aspirant demand; weak mobile/design (Blogger, stock WP, no dark mode); no self-serve media kit; domain-hygiene failures orphaning backlinks; no Hindi toggle; no community/polls.

**CTAs adopted:** named newsletter product (Daily Defence Brief); Support Us in nav; write-for-us; advertise landing with rate card; credibility strip; subscribe + gift; reader submissions; WhatsApp/Telegram follow buttons (India converts on WhatsApp); trending strip; appointments/procurement zones.

## C. Careers, industry & reference (14)
ssbcrack.com, ssbcrackexams.com, joinindianarmy.nic.in*, joinindiannavy.gov.in*, agnipathvayu.cdac.in, majorkalshiclasses.com, cavalierindia.com, idex.gov.in, sidm.in, ddpmod.gov.in, globalfirepower.com, aeroindia.gov.in, defexpo.gov.in*, militaryfactory.com

**Lead-gen forms observed:** MKC "Request Callback" (name, mobile, mode, state, category, course — best converter, WhatsApp fallback); Cavalier inquiry (name, phone, email, qualification, branch, address + "brochure emailed" reward); official portals use a single candidate profile reused across entries. **Adopted:** 2-step form — Step 1 exam chips + qualification + DOB + state → Step 2 name + WhatsApp mobile + email + mode; trust elements: free, ex-officer mentors, 24h callback, no spam.

**Exams/entries a careers hub must cover (implemented, 20):** NDA, CDS, AFCAT, SSB, TES, TGC, SSC Tech, NCC Special, JAG, Navy SSC/INET, Navy 10+2 B.Tech, CAPF AC, Territorial Army, ICG AC, ICG Navik/Yantrik, Agniveer Army, Agniveervayu, Agniveer Navy, SSC GD, AISSEE/RIMC.

**Vendor directory page should contain (implemented in data model):** company, type (DPSU/private/JV/start-up), state/corridor, domain taxonomy, products, listing/exchange; roadmap: certifications (ISO/AS9100/CEMILAC/DGQA), iDEX badges, RFQ button, tender history.

**Top interactive tools for repeat visits:** eligibility checker; exam calendar + countdown; cut-off predictor; mock tests/daily quiz; SSB timers; age calculator; salary/pension calculator; country comparison; equipment compare; tender/challenge tracker. Six shipped at launch; the rest are Phase-10 roadmap.

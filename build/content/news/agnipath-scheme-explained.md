Agnipath, launched in June 2022, replaced the traditional 17-year soldier enrolment with a **four-year tour of duty** across the Army, Navy and Air Force. Recruits are called Agniveers (Army), Agniveervayu (Air Force) and Agniveer SSR/MR (Navy).

## The money

| Year | Monthly package | In-hand (70%) | Seva Nidhi contribution (30%) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ₹30,000 | ₹21,000 | ₹9,000 |
| 2 | ₹33,000 | ₹23,100 | ₹9,900 |
| 3 | ₹36,500 | ₹25,580 | ₹10,950 |
| 4 | ₹40,000 | ₹28,000 | ₹12,000 |

The government matches the Agniveer's 30 percent contribution rupee-for-rupee. At exit the **Seva Nidhi package is about ₹10.04 lakh, tax-free**, plus interest. Agniveers also receive risk, hardship, dress and travel allowances at par with regular soldiers, a ₹48 lakh non-contributory life insurance cover, and — in case of death on duty — a ₹44 lakh ex-gratia plus pay for the unserved period.

## Retention: the 25 percent question

At the end of four years, up to **25 percent** of each batch is offered enrolment in the regular cadre for a further minimum of 15 years, based on merit, performance and organisational need. The remaining 75 percent exit with the Seva Nidhi, a skill certificate and Class 12 equivalence (for 10th-entry Agniveers, via NIOS).

## What the exits get

Central Armed Police Forces, Assam Rifles, Coast Guard, defence PSUs and several state police forces have announced 10 percent reservation for ex-Agniveers, along with age relaxation. Public-sector banks and some private groups have published preferential hiring policies. Whether these translate into jobs at scale is the key variable to watch — the first batch exits in 2026-27.

## Eligibility snapshot

* Age 17.5 to 21 years (a one-time relaxation to 23 applied in 2022 only).
* Army GD: Class 10 with 45 percent aggregate; Technical: Class 12 PCM; Clerk: Class 12 with 60 percent.
* Air Force: Class 12 with Maths, Physics and English (50 percent) or a three-year diploma.
* Navy SSR: Class 12 with Maths and Physics; MR: Class 10.

## Selection sequence

The Army moved its **Common Entrance Examination online and ahead of the rally** in 2023 — you sit the computer-based test first and are called to the rally only if you clear it. The Air Force's STAR exam and the Navy's INET-Agniveer follow the same online-first pattern.

> Practical advice: register the moment the notification appears, because Aadhaar-OTP-verified profiles and document uploads take time, and slots for the online test are allocated by region.

Women in the Indian Armed Forces — Every Entry Now Open, and What the Numbers Say

By DefenceIndia Desk · 2026-08-26 · 1 min read · Explainer
Advertisement

In a decade, the women's entry map has gone from a handful of short-service branches to almost every route a man can take.

The milestones

  • 2015 — women fighter pilots inducted in the IAF; over 20 now fly Su-30, MiG-29 and Rafale.
  • February 2020 — Supreme Court orders permanent commission for women SSC officers in the Army; Navy follows in March 2020.
  • August 2021 — Supreme Court opens the NDA to women; the first batch of 19 female cadets joins in 2022, and the first women cadets passed out in 2025.
  • 2022 — Sainik Schools and RIMC admit girls; Agniveer intakes include women in the Navy from the first batch and in the Army's Military Police.
  • 2023 — women officers posted to artillery regiments; the first woman commands a frontline Army unit.
  • 2024 — women admitted to the Army's 10+2 Technical Entry Scheme discussions and the Coast Guard's all-women crew sails.

Current strength (approximate)

Service Women officers Notes
Army ~1,700 (excluding medical) Plus ~100 in Corps of Military Police as other ranks
Navy ~700 All branches open incl. sea-going and submarine screening
Air Force ~1,600 Including fighter, transport and helicopter pilots
Coast Guard ~100 Women AC intake every cycle
CAPFs ~40,000+ CRPF and CISF lead; 33% reservation in constables in some forces

Entries open to women today

NDA, CDS (OTA, and IMA/INA/AFA via NDA route), AFCAT (all branches), SSC Tech, NCC Special Entry, JAG, Navy SSC (all branches), Coast Guard AC and Navik, Agniveer Navy SSR/MR, Agniveervayu, Agniveer Military Police, CAPF AC, SSC GD, Territorial Army.

What is still closed

The Army's infantry, armoured and mechanised infantry other-ranks streams remain men-only, and Agniveer GD for the Army is not yet open to women. These are policy decisions under review rather than legal bars.

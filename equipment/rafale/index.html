Dassault Rafale (IAF & Navy Rafale-M)

Twin-engine 4.5-gen multirole fighter · Indian Air Force / Indian Navy · Status: In service (36 IAF) / on order (26 Rafale-M)

Inducted in 2020 at Ambala and Hasimara, the Rafale brought Meteor beyond-visual-range missiles and SCALP cruise missiles to the IAF. The Navy's 26 Rafale-M will operate from INS Vikrant and INS Vikramaditya.

HAL Prachand (Light Combat Helicopter)

Attack helicopter · Indian Army & Indian Air Force · Status: In service; 156 on order (2025)

Designed for Siachen and Ladakh altitudes after Kargil, Prachand can take off and land at 5,000 m with weapons. The March 2025 order of 156 helicopters (₹62,700 crore) is HAL's largest ever.

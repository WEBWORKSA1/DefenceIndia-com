HAL Tejas Mk1A

Light multirole fighter · Indian Air Force · Status: In production

India's indigenous 4.5-generation light fighter, the Mk1A adds AESA radar, electronic warfare suite, air-to-air refuelling and BVR missiles. 83 aircraft ordered in 2021 with a follow-on order for 97 more, making it the numerical backbone of the IAF's future fleet.

Sukhoi Su-30MKI

Twin-seat heavy air-superiority / multirole fighter · Indian Air Force · Status: In service (~260)

The IAF's largest fighter fleet and the only aircraft in the world to carry the air-launched BrahMos. The 'Super Sukhoi' upgrade programme will add an indigenous AESA radar, new mission computer and EW suite to 84 aircraft.
